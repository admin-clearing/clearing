arXiv:2611.23398v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: The rapid scale-up of AI infrastructure has made datacenter reliability a cornerstone of the cloud business economy. Since hardware failures in hyper-scale AI clusters can stall multi-million dollar training workloads, the downtime of individual machines can cause outsized economic losses. Minimizing machine downtime, therefore, is more important than ever. To address this, we present Hardware Diagnoser (HardwareDx), an AI-driven hardware diagnosis system that applies a graph foundation model approach to automated datacenter maintenance. It allows a single model to diagnose many platforms and accelerate onboarding of new hardware products. Deployed at production scale at Google, HardwareDx yields substantial efficiency gains across diverse compute architectures. Real-world evaluations demonstrate that it reduces production downtime by 18.3% on GPU platforms and 16.1% on TPUs. Furthermore, it significantly accelerates hardware deployment, cutting quality assurance (QA) time by 28.3% for GPUs and 25.1% for TPUs. These results establish HardwareDx as a field-tested solution for maximizing the reliability and cost efficiency of next generation AI fleets.
