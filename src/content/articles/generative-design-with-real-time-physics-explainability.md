arXiv:2611.17560v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: In this article, we explore opportunities from using generative AI in mechanical engineering. On the one hand, generative methods based on invertible neural networks have been recently proposed for the solution of inverse design problems, allowing to infer design parameters from performance labels. On the other hand, generative AI models have shown that they can act as real time capable surrogates for physics based simulations. Here we suggest that these two streams should be combined in a unified framework, where INN are used to control the design process from the customer requirements perspective and the generative simulation of the systems physics adds a layer of explainability, why the proposed design fulfils the requirements. As generative design is prone to hallucinations, we propose uncertainty quantification to fend off physically infeasible requests. Finally, validation with classical tools is a requirement for generative design process. To demonstrate the capabilities of our novel generative design paradigm, we develop two interactive environments, one for airfoil design based on simulator data and one for the combustor design space exploration based on measured data.
